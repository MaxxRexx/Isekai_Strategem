# Triggers &amp; Black Triggers

40 regular Triggers (34 active, 6 passive) plus 10 Black Triggers form
the shared draft pool - any character may equip any of them regardless
of their own `CharacterType` (see [`GUIDE.md`](GUIDE.md) for Loadout
rules and Resonance). "Avg" below is the expected value of a Trigger's
damage or heal dice before Armor/resistances/Team Spirit/perk modifiers
are applied.

## Black Triggers

Each character may equip at most one. A Black Trigger can carry active
abilities (count toward the Loadout's required 4), passive abilities
(always on), and/or a **World ability** (a battle-wide effect, distinct
from a passive stat bonus). See
[`GUIDE.md`](GUIDE.md#resonance-how-well-a-black-trigger-suits-your-character)
for how well each type suits your character.

### Attack type

- **Ashbringer** (equip 45) - a high-damage melee/ranged pair with short
  cooldowns.
  - *Ashbringer Slash* (Attacker, Melee, Single, cooldown 1): Fire, avg
    60.0.
  - *Ashbringer Volley* (Shooter, Ranged, Single, cooldown 2): Fire, avg
    60.0.
- **Fracture Edge** (equip 42) - a burst attack plus offense-focused
  passives.
  - *Fracture Burst* (Shooter, Ranged, Burst - 3 hits, 2 targets,
    cooldown 2): Force, avg 25.5/hit.
  - Passives: +10 Critical Chance ("Fracture's Edge"), +6 Attack
    ("Sundering Focus").
- **Solar Flare** (equip 48) - a single massive AOE nuke on a long
  cooldown, plus a Critical Chance passive; the glass-cannon Black
  Trigger.
  - *Solar Flare* (Attacker, Ranged, AOE - 3 targets, cooldown 2):
    Radiant, avg 106.5.
  - Passive: +12 Critical Chance ("Blazing Focus").

### Defense type

- **Aegis Core** (equip 40) - World ability: negates the first 3
  instances of damage taken each battle entirely, regardless of size.
- **Bastion Frame** (equip 42) - Armor, Damage Resistance, and Status
  Resistance stack.
  - Passives: +8 Armor ("Bastion Plating"), Damage Resistance to
    Bludgeoning ("Hardened Shell"), +8 Status Effect Resistance
    ("Steady Mind").

### Support type

- **Wellspring** (equip 40) - a direct heal plus a Regenerating-granting
  ability.
  - *Wellspring Surge* (Optional, Melee, ally, Single, cooldown 2):
    Heal avg 5.5.
  - *Wellspring's Blessing* (Optional, Melee, ally, Single, cooldown 2):
    Inflicts Regenerating.
- **Chorus Bond** (equip 42) - Team Spirit and healing-focused passives,
  a suite for a dedicated support build.
  - Passives: +10 Team Spirit ("Bonded Resolve"), +6 Status Effect
    Infliction ("Harmonic Focus"), +6 Status Effect Resistance
    ("Warding Chorus"), +15 max health ("Vital Chorus").

### Unique type

- **Paradox Shard** (equip 44) - an unpredictable debuff ability paired
  with a disruptive psychic strike.
  - *Paradox Whisper* (Trapper, Psychic, Single, cooldown 2): Psychic,
    avg 21.5, inflicts Overwhelmed.
  - *Paradox Fracture* (Attacker, Psychic, Unique, cooldown 2): Psychic,
    avg 52.0.
- **Gravebind** (equip 38) - World ability: once per battle, survive a
  hit that would have reduced health to 0, landing on 1 instead.
- **Chrono Fragment** (equip 30) - World ability: increases the
  wielder's team's turn timer each turn - a tempo tool, not a combat
  one.
