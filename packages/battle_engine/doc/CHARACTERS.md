# Characters

20 characters, 5 per `CharacterType` (Attack/Defense/Support/Unique).
Every character has **100 max health** and a unique, always-on **perk**
independent of Loadout - it applies no matter what you equip, and often
interacts with whatever Triggers you draft (see
[`GUIDE.md`](GUIDE.md#status-effects-perks-and-black-triggers-interact-generically)).

Any character can equip any Trigger or Black Trigger regardless of
their own type - type only affects Black Trigger Resonance (see the
[Guide](GUIDE.md#resonance-how-well-a-black-trigger-suits-your-character)).

## Attack

| Name | Trion Cap. | Trion Aff. | Team Spirit | Armor | Attack | Defense | Crit% | FAT% | Status Infl./Res. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kaito Reyes | 110 | 20 | 35 | 1 | 27 | 8 | 15 | 12 | 8 / 3 |
| Vela Ashworth | 105 | 18 | 40 | 1 | 26 | 7 | 12 | 10 | 9 / 3 |
| Dross | 115 | 16 | 35 | 2 | 24 | 9 | 6 | 9 | 7 / 3 |
| Ren Kobayashi | 100 | 22 | 30 | 1 | 26 | 8 | 10 | 14 | 8 / 2 |
| Airi Tanaka | 100 | 20 | 45 | 1 | 22 | 9 | 13 | 11 | 9 / 4 |

- **Kaito Reyes - Last Ace.** Critical Chance is doubled while Kaito is
  the last living member of his team.
- **Vela Ashworth - First Blood.** Vela's first attack of the battle
  gains bonus Critical Chance if the target is at full health.
- **Dross - Overwhelm.** Dross's AOE abilities deal bonus damage to a
  target that already has an active status effect.
- **Ren Kobayashi - First Strike.** Ren gets a flat Attack bonus on the
  first ability he uses each battle.
- **Airi Tanaka - Feint.** The first attack made against Airi each
  battle is rolled with disadvantage.

## Defense

| Name | Trion Cap. | Trion Aff. | Team Spirit | Armor | Attack | Defense | Crit% | FAT% | Status Infl./Res. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marren Osei | 120 | 16 | 55 | 3 | 12 | 16 | 3 | 7 | 5 / 6 |
| Ilona Vance | 110 | 18 | 50 | 2 | 17 | 15 | 5 | 8 | 6 / 6 |
| Bastian Cole | 115 | 16 | 50 | 3 | 14 | 14 | 3 | 7 | 5 / 7 |
| Dorian Voss | 110 | 18 | 48 | 2 | 15 | 14 | 4 | 8 | 5 / 5 |
| Sable Whitlock | 115 | 18 | 55 | 3 | 14 | 15 | 4 | 8 | 5 / 6 |

- **Marren Osei - Bulwark.** Marren's Armor is doubled while any living
  teammate is below 25% health.
- **Ilona Vance - Riposte.** Whenever a melee attack against Ilona
  misses, she gains a stacking Attack buff for her next turn.
- **Bastian Cole - Absorb.** The first instance of damage Bastian takes
  each battle is reduced by half, independent of any World ability.
- **Dorian Voss - Immovable.** Dorian is immune to any effect that would
  reduce how many targets his ranged abilities can hit.
- **Sable Whitlock - Guardian's Instinct.** Once per battle, Sable can
  redirect an attack targeting a living teammate onto himself instead.

## Support

| Name | Trion Cap. | Trion Aff. | Team Spirit | Armor | Attack | Defense | Crit% | FAT% | Status Infl./Res. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Priya Nakamura | 105 | 24 | 65 | 1 | 10 | 10 | 4 | 9 | 10 / 5 |
| Soren Talvik | 105 | 20 | 60 | 1 | 12 | 9 | 5 | 9 | 16 / 5 |
| Yuki Amaral | 105 | 22 | 70 | 2 | 10 | 11 | 4 | 8 | 9 / 6 |
| Haru Ellison | 130 | 32 | 60 | 1 | 10 | 10 | 4 | 9 | 8 / 5 |
| Celestine Moreau | 110 | 20 | 62 | 2 | 10 | 11 | 4 | 8 | 10 / 6 |

- **Priya Nakamura - Combat Medic.** Priya's heal-over-time effects heal
  for more when applied to an ally below 50% health.
- **Soren Talvik - Weaken Resolve.** Status effects Soren inflicts last
  1 turn longer against a target already carrying one of his other
  debuffs.
- **Yuki Amaral - Devoted Aid.** Yuki receives 10% bonus to all healing
  regardless of source, including a drain-style Trigger that heals its
  user off someone else's health.
- **Haru Ellison - Battery.** While Haru is alive, her team's Trion gain
  roll gets a small positive modifier.
- **Celestine Moreau - Warding Presence.** Celestine's ally-targeted
  buffs also grant a bonus to Status Effect Resistance.

- **Zheng Anders - Foresight.** Once per battle, Zheng can reroll one of
  his own attack rolls.
- **Nadia Kessler - Chain Reaction.** Each additional ability Nadia uses
  in the same FAT-triggered turn deals more damage than the last.
- **Rurik Voss - All or Nothing.** Rurik's damage output rises as his
  own health drops, up to double at 0 health.
- **Mireille Song - Decoy.** Once per battle, an attack against Mireille
  has a chance to simply miss, as if she was never really there.
- **Tobias Renner - Versatile.** Tobias gets a small bonus to whichever
  stat matches the category of ability he last used.
