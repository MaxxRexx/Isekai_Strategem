What I bring to the table

In Naruto-Arena the whole game is the plan: three characters, four energy types trickling in, every skill a fixed cost and cooldown, and - critically - skills never miss. RNG lives only in which energy you draw. Skill expression is reading the opponent's cooldowns, baiting their invulnerability window, denying the energy they need for their kill turn, and chaining stuns so the target never acts. I judge a tactics game by one question: when my plan fails, was it my fault or the dice's?

What this game gets right (and it's a lot)

The counter / reactive layer is the most Naruto-Arena thing here, and it's excellent. Arming predictive_parry (dodge the next melee), guardians_aegis (guarded + braced), numbing_toxin (burst mitigation), or any of the 19 counters on your turn so they fire when the opponent commits into them - that is exactly the invuln/counter mind-game I live for. You're not just acting; you're setting traps for their action economy.

The unique triggers deepen it further. vow_of_the_duel (2× damage + a 2-turn stun on a bound target), karmic_bind, echoing_doubt, isolation, mindsEye reveal - these are the "signature jutsu" flavor, and the combo recognizer that rewards setup→payoff chains is precisely the coordinated-team-nuke fantasy. This is genuinely better designed than most of the genre.

Verdict from my chairThe counter/unique/combo spine is A-tier and it's what would keep me on the ladder. The problems below are all fixable tuning, not broken design.

The dealbreaker culture-clash: the d20 to-hit roll

My combos can whiff. In Naruto-Arena a planned three-skill kill lands, period - the counterplay is invuln/reduction, not a coin flip. Here every attack rolls Attack-die vs Defense-die, so a lethal setup can simply miss. To a chakra-game vet that feels like the game undoing my planning with its own dice.

What softens it - and this is important - is that in practice the die barely matters, because the flat stat modifiers dwarf the d20. A log line reads ATK d20:18 + 26 = 44 vs DEF d20:6 + 11 = 29. The ±20 of the die is swamped by a +26 vs +11 stat gap. So most attacks are near-auto-hits already. That's oddly reassuring to me - but it also means the roll is mostly theater: it adds a whiff-anxiety I dislike without adding real decisions. I'd rather you either commit to determinism (skills land; counters/invuln are the answer) or make the die actually swing outcomes. Right now it does neither cleanly.

Resource economy: close, but shallower than chakra

Trion is my chakra, and I like that abilities cost 10-30 and you bank a pool. But two things read as less deep than Naruto-Arena's four-energy puzzle:

  • It's one undifferentiated pool, not typed energy. In NA the tension is "I have the ninjutsu but not the taijutsu for my combo." Here any Trion pays for anything, so the denial/planning game is thinner. sapped (25% capacity drain) and genjutsu_trapped (15% drain) are the closest thing to energy-denial and I want more of that.
  • The gain is random (a tier roll for 10 / 20 / 35). NA's chakra gain is deterministic - one energy per living unit. Random income means my "next turn I can afford the finisher" math can just fail. For a planning-first player, income RNG is worse than combat RNG.
What I'd ask forMake Trion income deterministic (or a small, known range), and lean harder into Trion-denial as a counter axis. If income must be random, tie it tightly to the Trion Affinity stat so building for it is a real, reliable choice.

Tempo and cooldowns: where I smell the OP triggers

A tempo player's OP radar pings on low-cooldown, high-value, cheap tools - the ones that erase the cooldown puzzle. From the numbers, these stand out:

Contrast with the pure-control trapper kit (charm_whisper, mind_shatter, flashbang_round) that deals little or no damage. In my world those are fine if the CC is worth a turn. charmed at 3 turns is a genuine lock; most other CC is 1-2 turns, which is healthy. What I don't see is diminishing returns on repeated CC - if two operators can chain stun/charm the same target, that's a perma-lock, the cardinal sin of chakra-arena PvP.

The FAT valve - I respect it

FAT (up to 3 abilities in a turn, then doubled cooldowns and halved Trion Affinity for a 3-turn lockout) is a clean burst-tempo trade, and the fact that draegor punishes an opponent who FAT-chains 2+ abilities is exactly the kind of "greed gets punished" counter I want. Good.

My bottom line as a Naruto-Arena vet

  1. Keep the counter/unique/combo spine - it's your identity and it's great.
  2. Resolve the d20 identity crisis. Either commit to determinism (my preference) or make the die swing real outcomes. The current "roll that doesn't matter but can still whiff" is the worst of both.
  3. Deepen Trion: deterministic income, more denial tools, maybe a light energy-typing.
  4. Nerf the cd-1 spam (whirlwind_slash, twin_fang_strike) and the longshot one-shot; add CC diminishing returns so no two operators can perma-lock a target.
